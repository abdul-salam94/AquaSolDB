AquaSolDB viewer

Published measurements of 9 gases dissolved in water and brines, of the water in gas phases and of phase boundaries, from 499 sources (AquaSolDB v2.0, released 2026-10-08). It opens on CO₂; pick another gas under Filters. README · About the data

Start here. Pick a gas under Filters (on a phone, the rest is under More filters), then narrow the temperature, pressure and salt. The count, the plot and the table follow the filters; the plot can show any number column against any other, and a box dragged on it zooms in. Click a point or a row to see the value as printed in its source; click a column title to sort, its ⓘ for what it means. README
